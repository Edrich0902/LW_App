Pastoral blog · List + reader

Two directions for a quieter, more readable blog

Current cards feel samey and the reader dumps reactions as outlined pills under thin content. These concepts keep LWP blue + glass chrome, but treat pastoral writing like reading — not a feed of identical tiles.

B is selected. Use Has / No cover to see both image states. No reading time — meta is author · date · Amen only.

Featured + reader use the cover when one exists. Compact rows only show a thumb when that post has an image.

Same layout without photos: featured becomes a text stage; compact rows go full-width; reader opens on a title stage.

A · Quiet editorial: Drop the card chrome. List becomes a divider stream (title + excerpt + light meta). Reader leads with a slim cover band, strong title, and a sticky reaction dock — reactions stay available without competing with the words.

Pastorale brief

Wanneer stilte ook gebed is

Edrich Barnard
09 Aug 2026 · 3 min

Ons is so gewoond daaraan om te praat — selfs met God — dat stilte ongemaklik begin voel. Tog is daar oomblikke waar die siel nie nog ’n sin nodig het nie, maar ’n plek om asem te haal.

Stilte is nie die afwesigheid van geloof nie. Dit is dikwels die ruimte waarin geloof weer wortel skiet. Wanneer jy nie weet wat om te bid nie, mag jy by die Here sit soos ’n kind by ’n pa: sonder ’n agenda, met net teenwoordigheid.

Hierdie week nooi ek jou: skakel iets af. Sit vyf minute stil. Laat die Woord en die Gees die stilte vul. Jy hoef nie die stilte te “vul” nie — jy mag daarin rus.

B · Cover stage (locked in): Featured lead + compact stream + immersive reader when a cover exists. No reading time. When there is no image, featured becomes a text stage, compact rows drop the thumb, and the reader opens on a title stage — same hierarchy, no placeholder art.
